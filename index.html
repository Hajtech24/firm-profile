About

A career built on the numbers adding up

Hajras Professional Services is a consulting firm covering accounting, tax management, and financial advisory, working alongside Ajiboye Rasaq & Co, a Chartered Accountancy firm focused on audit and assurance. Both firms are led by Ajiboye Rasaq Opeyemi, Managing Partner — a Chartered Accountant, Auditor, Financial Analyst and IT Auditor with over a decade of experience across audit, accounting, taxation and business advisory.

He holds a B.Sc in Accounting from the University of Ilorin and an M.Sc in Accounting from the University of Lagos, alongside Fellowship qualifications from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (FCA) and the Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (FCTI). Alongside the day-to-day of running both firms, he's expanding into data science and web development — to bring sharper analytical and technical tools into the practice.
